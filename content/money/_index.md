A series about money in plain language — what it is for, how it behaves, and how to think about it — written for people for whom money is often tight, not for those who already have plenty.

Each article builds on the one before, so the numbers are the reading order. A **new article appears every Monday**; if you have arrived early, you have found the start of something rather than all of it.

## Not financial advice

The Money vault is general information, not personal financial advice. Drew Griffiths is not a regulated financial adviser, and nothing here is a personal recommendation, or a suggestion to buy, sell or hold any particular product or investment. Rules and figures change; check the current position against the official sources linked in each article before acting on anything. For a decision about your own circumstances, consider a regulated adviser.
