Gygax commentary & testimony corpus

Private research instrument. Absence from the recovered corpus is not evidence that something was never said — see corpus coverage.

Decrypted in your browser. The passphrase is never stored or transmitted; the database is held only in memory for this session, and evidence images are fetched and decrypted only when you open them.

Matches are reported in four separate dimensions: verified transcript (what was actually said), topic tag (our classification, matched exactly), question / context (words in the question, not the answer), and discovery text (unverified extraction, a finding aid only).

How to read a result

Speaker · relationship — who produced the testimony and how it reaches us: direct, direct quotation by intermediary, attributed report / paraphrase, eyewitness account, secondary interpretation, editorial or authorial inference, unattributed institutional statement, discovery only.

Transcript — Verified (checked against the evidence) or Untranscribed. Machine text is never transcript.

Evidence — the derivative shown by View source: direct screenshot, PDF reproduction, derivative crop, stitched derivative or scan. Original capture identifies the untouched original, which is held offline.

Discourse — commentary, retrospective commentary, in-world, or rules/game text.

A number shown as (inferred) was reconstructed from sequence and is not primary evidence.

Corpus coverage — what is present, partial and missing

A zero-result search means only that the term was not found in the recovered corpus. It is not evidence that the statement was never made.

Evidence images and original captures are held outside this site. Quoted source text is preserved verbatim and is never normalised or corrected. Tags are finding aids, not statements of fact.
